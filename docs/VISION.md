# Vision & message (shared by build and marketing)

## One line
Find stories shaped like the ones you love, on the services you already pay for.

## What Laminary is
A watch app that understands *how* stories are built. Every movie and series is read through four layers (Surface Story, Archetypal Plot, Mythic Blueprint, Structural Skeleton), so you can browse and match by story shape instead of genre.

## What it is not
- Not another "where is it streaming" utility. Availability is table stakes.
- Not an AI chatbot. The product is the structure, shown clearly.
- Not a critic. It explains the shape of a story. It doesn't grade it.

## Who it's for
People who pay for several streaming services, spend too long choosing, and know what they *felt* about a film better than which genre it was.

## Voice
Plain, specific, a little nerdy about stories, never hype. Say "Same arc as *Arrival*," not "AI-powered picks just for you." Never use the words "AI-powered," "magic," or "curated by algorithms" in the product.

## Promises the product must keep
1. Every recommendation says why, in one line.
2. Only shows what you can watch on your services.
3. Spoilers are hidden unless you choose to see them.
